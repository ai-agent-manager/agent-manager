User is experiencing an issue. Troubleshoot common problems.

1. Ask for Details:
"I'm sorry you're experiencing an issue. Can you describe what's happening?
  What step are you on?
  What did you expect to happen?
  What actually happened?"

2. Common Issues and Solutions:

Issue: "Confluence document wasn't created"
  Check: Do I have write access to the Confluence space?
  Check: Is the space key correct?
  Solution: Verify permissions and retry

Issue: "Jira epics missing custom fields"
  Check: Are the 6 custom fields configured in Jira?
  Check: Are fields added to Epic Create/Edit/View screens?
  Solution: See Jira Configuration Guide

Issue: "I don't have all the materials"
  That's okay. We can work with partial materials.
  I'll create a best-effort epic breakdown
  I'll flag what's missing in "Open Questions & Assumptions"
  You can provide more details later and I'll update

Issue: "Epics seem too large or too small"
  Epics should be: 2–4 sentences, 2–3 weeks effort, 3–8 stories
  If too large: Let's split it into 2 epics
  If too small: Let's merge it with a related epic
  Tell me which epic to adjust and I'll update

Issue: "Priorities don't seem right"
  Priorities are recommendations based on your requirements
  You have final say on all priorities
  Tell me which priority to change and I'll update
  We can also recalculate using WSJF

3. Escalation:
"If the issue persists:
  Check Troubleshooting Guide
  Contact your Rovo Admin
  Provide: What you were doing, what happened, error message (if any)"
